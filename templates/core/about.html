Our Mission & Aims

AccessAdvisr exists to give disabled people a stronger voice in shaping accessibility. We provide a platform where people can rate, review, and discuss the accessibility—or inaccessibility—of different places.

By sharing real experiences and first-hand information, we aim to make accessibility transparent and practical. Adding photographs helps others see the reality of access before they visit.

Transparency

No more hidden barriers. We reveal the true state of accessibility through real reviews.

Empowerment

Giving mobility-challenged individuals the tools to make informed decisions.

"Too often, accessibility is not quite what it seems. Ramps are sometimes more like ski slopes, and 'access' often means going through a service lift. AccessAdvisr was created out of that frustration."

Accessibility Statement

AccessAdvisr is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards. We believe it is our collective moral obligation to allow seamless, accessible, and unhindered use for those of us with disabilities.

Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. We aim to meet the WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards.

We aim to make all pages and content accessible, but some content may not yet fully meet the highest accessibility standards. This could be due to challenges in identifying the most suitable technological solution. We revise this statement periodically to reflect improvements to our practices.
